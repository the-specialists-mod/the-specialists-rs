## Description

TS `UTIL_ScreenShake` (0xec978) converts duration and frequency to fixed-point integers first. Then, for each player, it divides those integers by the player's time scale and truncates (0xecad3, 0xecb11), with no clamp after the division.

The port has two versions:
- `screen_shake` in `crates/server/src/stunt/mod.rs` divides the integers, but rounds half to even.
- `screen_shake` in `crates/server/src/entities/stock/explode.rs` divides the float before converting, and clamps to 0xffff.

Fix: keep one helper that truncates as TS does, and point the other at it (as #46 did for `EMIT_SOUND_DYN`).
