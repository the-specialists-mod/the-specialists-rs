## Description

TS `CBasePlayer::PreThink` (0x908cc) calls `WaterMove` (0xd905c) and `CheckTimeBasedDamage` (0xda00c).
- **`WaterMove`:** once `air_finished` passes, drowns the player (`DMG_DROWN`, damage rising from 2 to 5 per hit), counts the health lost to drowning (+0x9a8), and plays the `pl_wade` / `pl_swim` sounds.
- **`CheckTimeBasedDamage`:** every 2 s, handles the time-based damage bits that `TakeDamage` (0x8fa94) sets. Poison deals 2 per tick; `DMG_DROWNRECOVER` gives back up to 10 of the drowned health per tick.

The port only sets `air_finished` on spawn (`crates/server/src/rules/lifecycle.rs`). So players can't drown, poison `trigger_hurt`s leave no lingering damage, and drowned health never comes back. `crates/server/src/player/damage.rs` doesn't track time-based damage bits.
