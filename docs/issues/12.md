## Description

In TS, a corpse that is shot plays the bullet dance: `EnableBulletDance` triggers when the hit is
<= 80 damage, the body is slower than 80 u/s, and the hit isn't in the legs; `BulletDance` sets the
+0x1dc timer to 0.15. An airborne victim is also pushed by `dir*50`. The helpers exist
(`stunt::bullet_dance`, `enable_`/`disable_bullet_dance`), but nothing calls them.
