## Description

Found while working on #88 and #90 (checked in `ts_i386.so` and the port; Ghidra addresses):

1. **Close-combat flags.**
   - TS sets `glast_closecombat` only in `CloseCombatAttack` (CBasePlayer.c:4217) and `CheckForBreakables` (CTSStunt.c:1437), and sets `glast_coldcock` only in `ColdCockAttack` (CBasePlayer.c:4410).
   - The port guesses instead (`crates/server/src/rules/scoring.rs`), counting knife and katana slashes as close combat. So a slash kill never gets `#TS_KnifeKill`/`#TS_KatanaKill` (PlayerKilled 0x895e4), and gets the 80% motion-blur chance.
   - `#TS_Coldcock` is never sent.
2. **Death knockback.** `Killed` pushes the body with `-DamageForce × normalize(g_vecAttackDir)`, a direction computed from the inflictor's position (0x8f30f). The port uses the bullet's travel direction (the `dmg.dir * force` loop in `lifecycle.rs` `killed`).
3. **Damage to dead players.** In TS, a direct `TakeDamage` (fall, drown) on a player with `takedamage` off still resets the time-based damage and sends the director message and the fade. The port's `damage::apply` returns before reaching the player (`crates/server/src/damage.rs`, the non-traced path).
4. **Score message clamping.** TS's `AddPoints` (0xd9e18) sends `(int)frags` and keeps its low 16 bits. The port's `score_info_of` (`rules/lifecycle.rs`) uses `v.frags as i16`, which clamps instead.
5. **Cash copy at spawn.** On maps without a `game_player_equip`, TS's spawn copies `pl+0x540` into `pl+0x53c`. The port doesn't. Also check whether the multi-damage buffer's extra direction/position/weapon fields (#90) can follow TS's four arguments exactly.
