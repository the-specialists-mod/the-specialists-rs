## Description

TS `TSExplosion` (0x7d01c) has a branch at 0x7d678 for entities named `WorldGun` (decomp `global_00066308.c` ~4275–4378). It:
- calls `UTIL_SetOrigin` and sets the size to zero;
- switches the touch to `CTSWorldGun::BounceTouch` and the think to `CTSWorldGun::Fall` at +0.1 s;
- sets movetype 10 (bounce), solid 2, gravity 0.7 and friction 0.8;
- sets velocity to `normalize(gun − blast) × (r_max − dist) / weapon_info[+0x34] × 10` (a byte from `get_weapon_info`, 0x7d85c);
- if the gun is on the ground (`FL_ONGROUND` 0x200), clears the flag and sets `velocity.z = |velocity.z| + 50`;
- randomises `avelocity` y/z with `RandomLong(-300, 300)`.

The port:
- `crates/server/src/weapons/projectiles.rs::explode` adds a guessed `300 × away` plus 150 up, and keeps the gun's existing think/touch.
- `crates/server/src/entities/stock/explode.rs::ts_explosion`, which breakables use, doesn't knock WorldGuns at all.

The two ports of the same function also differ elsewhere: the damage coordinate in `TSFrags` is rounded ties-to-even in `projectiles` (TS uses `ROUND`) but truncated in `explode.rs`, and only `explode.rs` guards against `r_max == r_full`.

Fix: port the WorldGun branch exactly, and keep a single `TSExplosion` that both the grenade and `CBreakable::Die` (0x752e0) call.
